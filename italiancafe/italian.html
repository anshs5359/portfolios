Our Story

A Family Recipe, Carried Across Borders

Amore began in a small kitchen in Bologna, where Nonna Lucia rolled pasta by hand every morning before sunrise. In 2014, her grandson brought that same recipe book to Sector 24 — along with her copper pots, her stubbornness about "proper" tomatoes, and her belief that a meal should make you feel at home.

Today, our kitchen still rolls dough by hand and pulls espresso the slow way. Every plate that leaves our pass carries a little of that Bologna morning with it.
