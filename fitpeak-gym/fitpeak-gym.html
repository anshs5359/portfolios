Daily Wisdom

Workout Tips

Smart training beats hard training. Here are this week's expert tips from our coaches.

💧 Hydration
Drink water before you feel thirsty
By the time you feel thirsty you're already 2% dehydrated, which reduces performance by up to 20%. Aim for 500ml of water 30 minutes before every workout.
😴 Recovery
Muscles grow when you sleep, not when you lift
Aim for 7–9 hours of sleep per night. Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep and is essential for muscle repair and fat metabolism. Sleep is not optional.
🥗 Nutrition
Eat protein within 30 minutes post-workout
The anabolic window is real. Consuming 20–40g of fast-absorbing protein (like whey or eggs) immediately after training maximises muscle protein synthesis.
📈 Progressive Overload
Add 2.5kg every week for consistent gains
The number one rule of strength training: do more than last time. Track your lifts every session and aim to add weight or reps weekly for steady strength progression.
🧘 Flexibility
Warm up dynamically, stretch statically after
Dynamic stretching before lifts primes muscles and joints. Save static holds for post-workout when muscles are warm — this is when flexibility actually improves.
🧠 Mindset
Consistency beats intensity every time
Four moderate workouts per week for a year beats two brutal sessions per week. Build habits that are sustainable. Results are a byproduct of showing up reliably.
