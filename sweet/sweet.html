A Legacy of Pure Taste

Aggarwal Sweets & Snacks is a well-recognized name in the hospitality industry in Rohini, Delhi. Located Near Iskcon Temple, Rohini Sector –25, Delhi, we have been serving our community with quality, pure, and authentic Indian sweets for decades.

We believe food should nurture the heart. Every sweet that leaves our kitchen is prepared with pure desi ghee, carefully selected ingredients, and a recipe perfected across generations. Our signature creations — Kaju Katli, Dhoda, and Kalakand — are made using a special benchmarked recipe passed down through time.

Over the years, Aggarwal Sweets has grown to be synonymous with premium quality Indian sweets. Using technique, skill, and the finest raw materials sourced from across India, our mithai artisans craft each batch with noticeable freshness you can feel, taste, and enjoy.
