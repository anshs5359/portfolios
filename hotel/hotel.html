About FabHotel Rama

Your Home Away From Home in Delhi

FabHotel Rama is a well-established hotel nestled in the heart of Rohini, one of New Delhi's most well-connected residential and commercial areas. We combine modern comforts with warm Indian hospitality to create an experience that keeps our guests coming back.

Whether you're visiting for business, family, or leisure, our thoughtfully designed rooms and attentive staff ensure every stay is memorable. Enjoy the convenience of metro connectivity, safe surroundings, and a team dedicated to your comfort 24×7.
