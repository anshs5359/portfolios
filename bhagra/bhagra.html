FAQs

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know before stepping into the studio.

Do I need any dance experience to join?

Not at all! Our Beginner Bhangra and Bollywood Fitness classes are designed for complete beginners. Our trainers start from scratch and make sure everyone picks up the moves at their own pace.

What should I wear and bring to class?

Wear comfortable, breathable activewear and sport shoes (preferably dance sneakers or cross-trainers). Bring a water bottle, a small towel, and your energy!

How many calories does a typical class burn?

A 60-minute Bhangra Fitness class typically burns between 400–700 calories depending on your weight and intensity level. It's one of the most enjoyable ways to achieve a serious calorie deficit.

Can I freeze or pause my membership?

Yes! Quarterly, Half-Yearly, and Annual members can freeze their membership for up to 30 days per year due to travel, illness, or personal reasons. Just notify the front desk 48 hours in advance.

Are there classes for children?

Absolutely! Our Kids Dance Fitness classes are designed for children aged 5–14. These sessions focus on coordination, rhythm, and fun — making fitness a habit from a young age.

Is the studio hygienic and safe?

Absolutely. The studio is deep-cleaned daily, equipped with CCTV cameras, and we maintain a strict zero-harassment policy. We also provide sanitiser stations throughout the studio.

What is the free trial class?

We offer a complimentary one-time trial class to all new visitors. Just show up 10 minutes early to any batch, and our front desk team will help you get started. No commitment required!
