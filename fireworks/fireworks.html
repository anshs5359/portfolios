Fireworks Safety Guidelines

Fireworks can cause serious injury and fire. Please read these guidelines before you buy, and again before you light anything.

  • Purchase only permitted products.

  • Follow local laws and regulations.

  • Follow the instructions printed on the product.

  • Keep fireworks away from children and flammable materials.

  • Use fireworks only in permitted, open areas.

  • Keep water or sand nearby for emergencies.

  • Never hold lit fireworks in your hand.

  • Never attempt to relight a failed firework.

Legal & Compliance

Availability, sale, storage, transportation and use of fireworks may be subject to applicable Indian central, state and local laws and regulations. These can include licensing requirements and restrictions on the types of products, the places where they may be used and the times of day they may be used.

Rules differ between states and districts and can change, so please check with your local authorities before you order. Shree Balaji Crackers may decline or cancel an order that cannot be lawfully fulfilled. The information on this website is general guidance and is not legal advice.
